# Mines Game – Build Instructions

Create a Mines-style betting game with a clean, modern casino interface.

## Game Overview
Mines is a risk-and-reward game played on a grid.  
The player reveals tiles one by one, trying to avoid hidden mines while increasing a payout multiplier.

## Objective
Allow the player to place a bet, reveal safe tiles, increase winnings, and cash out before hitting a mine.

## Core Rules
- The player has a balance.
- The player selects a bet amount.
- The player selects the number of mines before starting.
- A fixed-size grid of tiles is generated.
- Mines are randomly hidden behind tiles.
- Tiles start hidden.
- The player clicks tiles to reveal them.

## Tile Outcomes
- **Safe tile**:
  - Increases the multiplier.
  - The tile visually changes to indicate success.
  - The game continues.
- **Mine tile**:
  - Ends the round immediately.
  - The entire bet is lost.
  - All mines are revealed.

## Multiplier Logic
- The multiplier increases with each safe tile revealed.
- More mines selected = faster multiplier growth.
- Multiplier is clearly displayed and updated in real time.

## Cash Out Rule
- The player can cash out at any time **before** hitting a mine.
- Cashing out:
  - Ends the round.
  - Pays out: bet × current multiplier.
  - Locks in winnings safely.

## Game Flow
1. Player sets bet amount.
2. Player selects number of mines.
3. Player starts the round.
4. Grid becomes active.
5. Player reveals tiles one by one.
6. Multiplier increases on each safe tile.
7. Player either:
   - Cashes out and wins, or
   - Hits a mine and loses.
8. Round ends and balance updates.

## UI Layout
### Grid Area
- Square grid (e.g. 5×5).
- Tiles are evenly spaced.
- Tiles are clickable and animated on reveal.

### Information Area
- Current multiplier display.
- Current potential payout display.
- Number of mines selected.

### Control Area
- Balance display.
- Bet amount controls (increase/decrease).
- Mine count selector.
- Start button.
- Cash out button (enabled only during an active round).

## Visual Style
- Dark background.
- Neon or soft glow accents.
- Clear win and loss states.
- Smooth tile flip or reveal animations.
- Winning tiles feel rewarding.
- Mines are visually distinct and dramatic when revealed.

## Behavior Constraints
- Mines positions remain fixed during a round.
- A revealed tile cannot be clicked again.
- Only one round can be active at a time.
- Balance cannot go negative.
- Cash out is disabled after a mine is triggered.

## Experience Goals
- High tension with each click.
- Clear risk vs reward.
- Immediate visual feedback.
- Fast, responsive gameplay.
- Mobile and desktop friendly.

Build the complete playable game strictly following this description.
